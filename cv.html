Hitanshu Pandit, Ph.D.

Visiting Assistant Professor of Economics, Bates College · Visiting Research Fellow, Dukakis Center for Urban and Regional Policy, Northeastern University

I am an applied economist working on labor, health and urban questions. I build measures of work from large data that was never collected for research, such as job postings, worker profiles, mobile-device visits, card transactions and Medicaid claims, and I use them to answer questions that cities, state agencies and firms are already asking.
